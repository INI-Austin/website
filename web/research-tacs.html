Research / Stimulation

Non-invasive tACS

How reproducible are the reported effects of 40 Hz transcranial alternating current stimulation on memory measures?

Why this is open

The headline results are well known. The replication record is less well known, and the two are usually cited together without distinguishing them.

Approach

Effect sizes and study characteristics extracted from published trials, with pre-registration status and sample size recorded for each. The chapter runs no stimulation of any kind.

Deliverable

A dataset of extracted effect sizes, released openly, plus a poster for the spring showcase.
