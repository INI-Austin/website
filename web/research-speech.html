Speech and memory

Which speech and language measures reported as early signals in Alzheimer's disease have been validated in more than one cohort?

Why this is open

Dozens of measures are proposed. Far fewer are tested twice. The gap between those two numbers is the finding.

Approach

A systematic map of published measures against the cohorts they were tested in, using only public data and published summary statistics.

Deliverable

A public map of measures to cohorts, and a manuscript submitted to an undergraduate research journal.
