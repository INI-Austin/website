The University of Texas at Austin

A research group, not a club.

INI Austin is the UT Austin chapter of the Institute of Neuro Innovation. Ten to twenty-five Research Fellows work a named question with a named lead and a written scope, across two to three projects a year, and the chapter publishes what comes out of it. No dues, at any of the three membership tiers.

How the chapter is built

Three tiers, open to any UT student in any major and any class year. Two of them are by application, and they lead to different places.

  1. General Member

    No application. Register and attend the speaker series, workshops, and brain health programming. No obligation, no dues.

  2. Committee Member

    By application. Serve on a standing committee under a Vice President. This is the leadership track: it is the principal pathway to becoming that Vice President and potentially President.

  3. Research Fellow

    By application, reviewed by the President of Research. Open to every class year. Assigned to a project team, with a written progress report to your Research Lead one day before each weekly work meeting. This is the research track.
